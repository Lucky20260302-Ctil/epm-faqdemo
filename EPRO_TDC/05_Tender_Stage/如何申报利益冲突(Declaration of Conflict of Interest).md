## 6.2 Tender Stage

No supplier submission can be reviewed before buyer completing the DOI, DOI e-Form is auto-
generated for assigned users who are involved in the RFQ / Tender process.

**Step 1:** Buyer selects the relevant e-Form under “Drafts” section.

**Step 2:** Check the box to identify no conflict of interest with tenderer / bidder from the
invitation list, or;

Fill in the remarks if any existing / potential conflict of interest the buyer is aware of.

**Step 3:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 4:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 5:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

Buyer can review and proceed RFQ / Tender related action (e.g. compliance verification for
NDA / Briefing) after submission.

Buyer is required to obtain further approval for DOI with conflict of interest that stated in
section 6.2.1.1.

**Step 1:** Draft e-Form of DOI approval is auto-generated upon declaration.

Previous declared details is available for review, and it requires buyer to input the proposed
resolution and identify if above conflict of interest has been declared in the HR system in
advance.

**Step 3:** Select the approval route from dropdown menu based on the staff grade of declared
user, exceptional approval is required and specified with the approval details (Refer to section

### 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission.

Declared user can continue to patriciate in related RFQ / Tender task upon approval
completion, otherwise buyer is required to assign another user for further proceeding.
