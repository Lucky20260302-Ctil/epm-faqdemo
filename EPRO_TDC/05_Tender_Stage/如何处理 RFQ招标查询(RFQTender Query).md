Tenderer / bidder is allowed to submit RFQ / Tender query before specific closing date time
being pre-set.

Buyer is required to complete the compliance verification for submitted query(ies),
disqualification may occur if any price information is included with any query(ies).

Buyer shall issue the query response to all qualified tenderer / bidder.

**Step 1:** Select the relevant e-Form auto-generated upon supplier submission under “Drafts”
section.

**Step 2:** Download and review the submitted query(ies) from supplier.

**Step 3:** Click to confirm there is no pricing or sensitive information included with submitted
query(ies).

**Step 4:** Indicate the compliance result, tenderer / bidder who is disqualified is unable to access
the RFQ / Tender details.

**Step 5:** Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

**Step 6:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 7:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 8:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission.

**Step 1:** Buyer creates a new e-Form for query response under “New Requests” section,
selecting the relevant RFQ / Tender to proceed.

**Step 2:** Specify the recipient of this query response, either all or individual tenderer / bidder.

**Step 3:** Upload document and fill in the information for query response.

**Step 4:** Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

**Step 5:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 7:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 8:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission, query response

> **Email:** is issued to selected tenderer / bidder automatically with an email notification upon approval
completion.
