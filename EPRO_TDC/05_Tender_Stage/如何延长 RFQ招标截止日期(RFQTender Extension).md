Buyer can extend the RFQ / Tender closing date time before the specific closing date time being
pre-set.

**Step 1:** Buyer creates a new e-Form for RFQ / Tender extension under “New Requests”section,
selecting the relevant RFQ / Tender to proceed.

**Step 2:** Select the new proposed closing date time and fill in reason(s) of delay for approver’s
reference.

**Step 3:** Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission, email
notifications of extension closing date time are sent to all tenderers / bidders automatically
upon approval completion.
