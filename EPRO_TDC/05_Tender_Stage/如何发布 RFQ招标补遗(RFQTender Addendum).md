6.2.5  RFQ / Tender Addendum


Buyer can issue the RFQ / Tender addendum to either all or specific tenderer / bidder before
the specific closing date time being pre-set.

Step 1: Buyer creates a new e-Form for addendum under “New Requests” section, selecting the
relevant RFQ / Tender to proceed.





Step 2: Upload document and fill in the additional information.

Step 3: Specify the reason(s) of addendum issuance for approver’s reference.






Step 4: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 5: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 7: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.
