5.2 Supplier Invitation – Pre Reg


Before initiating any RFQ or Tender exercise, new suppliers must be invitedand registrated
through the system. .


    5.2.1   Issue Supplier Pre-Registration Invitation


Step 1: Requestor creates a new e-Form for pre-registration invitation under “New Requests”.





Step 2: Fill in the supplier name, contact email and number, and reason for invitation.






The Requestor can link up a drafted PS e-Form for approver’s reference by clicking “Select
Procurement Strategy” if necessary.






System provides duplication checking on supplier name, message will pop for reminder if
duplicate supplier record found, but the Requestor can still proceed to invitation.






Step 3: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, specify the approval details (Refer to
section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request will be sent to selected approver after submission.

Upon approval, the supplier receives an invitation email with account activation link . A drafted
pre-registration form is created in the system. Supplier status updates to “Registering”.


5.2.2  Response Checking for Supplier Pre-Registration


Response checking is mandatory for supplier registration. An email is sent to the Requestor
after supplier submission while the response check e-Form auto-generated as draft in the
system.

Step 1: The Requestor logs in to the system and click to open the response checking e-Form
under “Draft”.





Step 2: Review the supplier details.

Step 3: Verify the company address by checking the box or input additional information.






Step 4: Select “Compliance” to complete the pre-registration; or

Select “Non-Compliance” with explanation to return the e-Form for supplier’s update.

Step 5: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 6: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 7: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

The supplier receives a confirmation email for registration completed and supplier status is
updated to “Potential Supplier”. The supplier can be added to the invitation list of PS e-Form.
