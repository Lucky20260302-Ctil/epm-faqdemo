5.4 Supplier Information Change

    5.4.1  Supplier Information Change
This process allows the Requestor to update supplier registration details in the system, which
will be synced to FMS upon approval.



Step 1: The Requestor creates new e-Form for information change for the relevant supplier
under “New Requests” section.





Step 2: Select the supplier whose information needs to be updated.

Step 3: Input the updated details into the supplier registration form.

Step 4: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 5: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.


Step 6: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 7: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

Supplier information is updated in the system and changes are synchronized with FMS upon
approval completion.

Step 1: The Requestor creates a new e-Form of BR change for the relevant supplier under “New
Requests” section.





Step 2: Select the relevant supplier.

Step 3: Update the BR details including expiry date, certifcate number or upload new document

5.4.3  Supplier Merge


In view of existing supplier might registered and submitted a new EPRO full-registration
account and causing a duplicate record. This process is used to allow user to merge duplicate
supplier records, especially when a supplier as mistakenly submitted a new full-registration
account..

Step 1: User creates a new e-Form of supplier merge for the relevant supplier under “New
Requests” Section.





Step 2: Select the potential supplier by          .(newly submitted full-registration)

Step 3: Select the existing registered / approved supplier to be merged with      .







Step 4: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 5: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 6: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 7: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the E-form”.

The full registration form submitted by the potential supplier is removed and data is merged
into the existing registered / approved supplier profile with updated information reflected in
the system upon approval completion.






5.4.4  Supplier Termination
The process is used to terminate the supplier accounts, preventing them from further accessing
to the system.

Step 1: User creates a new e-Form of supplier termination under “New Requests” section.





Step 2: Download the excel template to review existing terminated / masked supplier.






Step 3: Access to the excel file and add supplier information for temination, save and close the
updated file .





Step 4: Upload the updated excel to the system with effective date and reason of termination.






Step 5: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 6: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 7: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 8: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

Supplier status is updated to “Terminated” and supplier is unable to log in or access the system.
