## 5.3 Supplier Invitation – Full Reg

To proceed Tender or RFQ award, user must invite supplier for full registration.

**Step 1:** The Requestor creates a new e-Form for pre-registration invitation under “New
Requests”.

**Step 2:** Select the supplier from the list.

Supplier information will be automatically filled from the supplier pre-registration.

**Step 3:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 4:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 5:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

Supplier receives an invitation email with account activation link with a drafted full
registration e-Form created in the system.

The suppliers who are registered or approved in FMS only are auto-synced into the system as
“FMS Only” type. User can invite those suppliers for PS e-Form after supplier account activation
in EPRO.

**Step 1:** Add supplier with supplier type of “FMS Only” under the supplier list in PS e-Form Part
I.

Supplier can be filtered by “FMS Only” or input keywords from the search filter.

**Step 2:** Select the supplier and confirm.

**Step 3:** System auto-generates Account Activation e-Form as draft with reminder message of
“Invite successfully. Please go to “My Task” > “Drafts” > “Full Registration Supplier Activation”
to fill-in supplier e-mail address for the account activation.”. The Requestor selects relevant e-
Form under “Drafts” section. .

**Step 4:** Input supplier’s email address to send out invitation.

**Step 5:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 6:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 7:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An Activation email is sent to selected supplier automatically with a drafted full registration e-
Form created in the system upon submission.

User can activate the EPRO supplier account for supplier type of FMS Only (except for those
who are in Terminated / Suspended / Masked status).

**Step 1:** Access to supplier list and select relevant supplier

**Step 2:** Click “Create Full Registration Supplier Activation”.

System auto-generates Account Activation e-Form as draft with reminder message of “Invite
successfully. Please go to “My Task” > “Drafts” > “Full Registration Supplier Activation” to fill-in
supplier e-mail address for the account activation.”.

**Step 3:** The Requestor selects relevant e-Form under “Drafts” section.

**Step 4:** Input supplier’s email address to send out invitation.

**Step 5:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 6:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 7:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An Activation email is sent to selected supplier automatically with a drafted full registration e-
Form created in the system upon submission.

Response checking is a mandatory step in the supplier full registration process. After the
supplier submits the full registration form, an email is sent to the Requestor while a response
check e-Form is auto-generated as a draft in the system.

**Step 1:** Log in to the system and click to open the response checking e-Form under the “Drafts”
section.

**Step 2:** Review the supplier details.

**Step 3:** Verify the checklist items by checking the verification box, or inputting additional
information if necessary.

**Step 4:** Select “Compliance” to proceed with full registration, or;

Select “Non-Compliance” with explanation to return the e-Form for supplier’s update.

**Step 5:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 6:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 7:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the E-form”.

Compliance full registration is sent to FMS for supplier create / update request, supplier status
is updated to Registered / Approved supplier after approval completion in FMS.

When supplier stated a conviction of offences for their registration as below:

An exception approval will be required before submitting to FMS. Refer to section 4.2.2.2 for
approval settings.
