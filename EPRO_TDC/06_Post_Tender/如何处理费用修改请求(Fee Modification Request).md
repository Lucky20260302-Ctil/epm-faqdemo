Buyer can submit approval request to modify fees and/or total amounts after Post-Tender
Clarifications.

**Step 1:** Buyer creates a new e-Form for fee modification under “New Requests” section,
selecting the relevant RFQ / Tender to proceed.

Select the supplier for modification.

**Step 2:** Input modification details, including: •  Updated Total Quote Price; •  Upload document related to cost proposals; •  Provide reason(s) of any change(s) and supplement clarification response details;

**Step 3:** Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission, and the total
quote price is updated for supplier upon approval completion.
