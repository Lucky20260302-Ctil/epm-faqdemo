6.3.8    RFQ / Tender Award Recommendation


     6.3.8.1     E-Form for Award Recommendation


Step 1: Buyer creates a new e-Form for award recommendation under “New Requests” section,
inputting or selecting the relevant RFQ / Tender by   to proceed.






Step 3: Input details for award recommendation, including:

   •  Description of PO Creation;
   •  Award supplier – only supplier who passed the technical assessment and endorsed by
       overall assessment result is available for selection from dropdown list;
   •   Site Name – option(s) is/are available to select from the dropdown list once the award
       supplier is chosen;
   •   Selection Reason;
   •  Declaration – Remark field is mandatory if “No” is selected;

Upload Attachment for PO.





Step 4: Finalize the PO line items by     button, line items are imported from previous data in
Procurement Initiation e-Form in section 6.1.2.






Update the quantity (default as 0), unit price and upload related document by “Edit” button if
needed.






Step 5: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 6: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.






Step 7: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 8: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission, PO creation
request is automatically submitted to FMS upon successful approval.






Buyer can review the submitted award recommendation e-form under “Sourcing”.





The e-Form presented a snapshot of previously submitted records; any updates made
afterward will not be reflected on, e.g. the PO number and PO status.

To review the completed purchase order details, users should click the 'Master Info' button,
where all related information is available.
