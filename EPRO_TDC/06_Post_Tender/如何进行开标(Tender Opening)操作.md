A drafted e-Form for unlock tender box is auto-generated for assigned opening team members
after the RFQ / Tender closing date time.

**Step 1:** Opening team members select the relevant e-Form under “Drafts” section.

**Step 2:** Review the brief information of RFQ / Tender for confirmation.

**Step 3:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 4:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 5:** Review and confirm the terms and condition.

**Step 6:** Click “Request One-time Password (OTP)” to complete the validation, OTP is received

> **Email:** from a email notification.

**Step 7:** Click “Yes, proceed with submitting the E-form” to complete the unlock.

All opening team members are required for OTP validation before proceeding to record of RFQ
/ Tender submission.

Record of RFQ / Tender opening e-Form allows opening team members to update the offline
submission for tenderer / bidder, supplementary sample or additional information, or even
disqualify tenderer / bidder who has included the inappropriate pricing information in the
technical folder in their submission.

**Step 1:** A drafted record of technical opening is auto-generated for opening team members and
it requires one (1) of the team members to submit the submission record.

**Step 2:** Upload the sample offline submitted by tenderer / bidder by  button with submission
date if needed.

**Step 3:** Download and review the technical proposal submitted from tenderer / bidder.

**Step 4:** Specify the compliance result, tenderer / bidder who is disqualified isexcluded from
assessment stage.

**Step 5:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 6:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review

**Step 7:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

**Step 8:** Endorsement for RFQ / Tender technical opening record is auto-generated for opening
team members in “Drafts” section.

A complete record is required for ALL opening team mebmers for endorsement.

**Step 9:** Select the endorsemen result, the opening record is returned for updating and being re-
submitted only if one of the team members select the result as “Not endorse”.

**Step 10:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 11:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 12:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

> **Email:** An email notification is sent to Buyer upon successful endorsement from all opening team
members.
