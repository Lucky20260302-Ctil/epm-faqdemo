**Step 1:** Buyer creates a new e-Form for supplier performance evaluation under “New Requests”
section, selecting the relevant RFQ / Tender to proceed.

**Step 2:** Input details negotiation, including: •  User for Performance Review; •  Supplier for Review; •  The Commence Date and Expected Completion Date; • Select the performance review template; • Identify the service nature and any disclosure of HKTDC’s confidential information.

**Step 3:** Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for performance request is sent to the selected approver after submission.

> **Email:** A draft for performance review e-Form is auto-generated with an email notification to selected
reviewer automatically upon approval completion.

**Step 1:** A draft of Supplier Performance Evaluation e-Form is auto-generated under “Drafts”
section.
