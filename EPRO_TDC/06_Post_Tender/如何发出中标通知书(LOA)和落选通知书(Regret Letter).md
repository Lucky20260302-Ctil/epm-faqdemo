6.3.10     Letter of Acceptance (LOA) and Unsuccessful / Regret Letter


    6.3.10.1     Issue LOA or Unsuccessful / Regret Letter


Step 1: Buyer creates a new e-Form for LOA or Unsuccessful / Regret Letter issuance under
“New Requests” section, selecting the relevant RFQ / Tender and supplier to proceed.






6.3.10.2    For LOA:


Step 2: Input LOA details, including:

   •  Deadline for supplier return;
   •  Upload LOA document.





Step 3: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.


Step 6: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission, LOA is issued
to the selected supplier automatically with an email notification upon approval completion.


    6.3.10.3    For Unsuccessful / Regret Letter:


Step 2: Input unsuccessful / regret letter details, including:

   •  Supplier for issuance;
   •   Select email template for supplier notification (Default is ‘Yes’ for the pre-defined
       template)
   •  Upload unsuccessful / regret letter document (select ‘No’ for Use of Template Email).

Step 3: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 4: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 5: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.



An email for approval request is sent to selected approver after submission, and negotiation is
issued to selected supplier automatically with an email notification upon approval completion.






6.3.10.4     Signed Letter of Acceptance Checking


Step 1: A draft of LOA checking e-Form is auto-generated under “Drafts” section upon response
by supplier.





Step 2: Download the signed document submitted by supplier.

Step 3: Upload compliance document, identify the submission method and supplement details
by remark field.

Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.
