## 6.3 Post Tender Stage

Buyer is required to assign the opening team members before the start of RFQ / Tender
opening.

A draft for assigning opening team members is auto-generated in “Drafts” section for Buyer
after the Issuance of RFQ / Tender.

**Step 1:** Buyer selects the relevant e-Form under “Drafts” section.

**Step 2:** Assign minimum of three (3) of opening team members by button or input user
names for searching.

**Step 3:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 4:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 5:** Confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.

A draft for declaration of interest is auto-generated for the assigned users with email
notifications (Refer to section 6.2.1 for DOI).
