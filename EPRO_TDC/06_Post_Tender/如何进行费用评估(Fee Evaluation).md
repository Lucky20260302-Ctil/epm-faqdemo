    6.3.5     Fee Evaluation


     6.3.5.1     Unlock Tender Box


For RFQ / Tender in Two-Envelope Approach, a drafted e-Form for unlocking tender box is
auto-generated for opening team members upon successful endorsement of technical
assessment result, allowing them to unlock the tender box for fee proposal by OTP validation
and records of pricing submission.

Only tenderer(s) / bidder(s) who passed the technical assessment is/ are able to unlock pricing
proposal(s).



For RFQ / Tender in Single-Envelope Approach, a drafted e-Form for unlocking tender box is
atuo-generated for opening team members after the closing date time.



Refer to section 6.3.2.1 for detailed steps of unlocking tender box.


     6.3.5.2      Tender Opening


Step 1: A drafted record of tender opening is auto-generated for opening team members and it
requires one (1) of the team members to submit the submission record after tender box unlock.






Step 2: Upload the sample offline submitted by tenderer / bidder by  button with submission
date if needed.






Step 3: Download and review the pricing proposal submitted from tenderer / bidder, fill in the
remarks to supplement additional information if needed.

Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review



Step 6: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

Step 7: Endorsement for RFQ / Tender opening record is auto-generated for opening team
members in “Drafts” section.






A complete record is required with ALL opening team mebmers for endorsement.

Step 8: Select the endorsemen result, the opening record is returned for updating (requiring
reason(s) of return) and being re-submitted only if one of the team members select the result
as “Not endorse”.

Step 9: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 10: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 11: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email notification is sent to Buyer upon successful endorsement from all opening team
members.



6.3.5.3       Start Tender / RFQ Fee Evaluation Request


Step 1: Buyer can start fee evaluation upon successful endorsement of tender opening for
pricing proposals under “New Requests” section.







Step 2: Input the price evaluation date.

Step 3: Select the price assessor for further proceeding.

Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email notification is sent to the selected assessor for notifying the pricing proposals which
are available for access and download after submission.






6.3.5.4      Access Fee Proposal


Assigned price assessor(s) receive(s) email notification(s) after the fee evaluation initiated by
Buyer from section 6.3.5.

Pricing proposals can be reviewed from “Completed Tasks” section with e-Form of “View RFQ /
Tender Price File”.





Assessor can click to download the pricing proposals submitted by tenderer(s) / bidder(s), or
review the filled pricing schedule e-Form for evaluation.
