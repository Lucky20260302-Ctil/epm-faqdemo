6.3.9      Negotiation


     6.3.9.1      Issue Negotiation


Step 1: Buyer creates a new e-Form for post-tender negotiation under “New Requests” section,
selecting the relevant RFQ / Tender to proceed.






Step 2: Input details for negotiation, including:

   •  Closing date for supplier submit negotiation;
   •  Upload negotiation document;
   •  Supplier of negotiation – Selection up to one (1) supplier for each negotiation request;
   •  Remarks (Optional).





Step 3: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.






Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission, and
negotiation is issued to the selected supplier automatically with an email notification upon
approval completion.






6.3.9.2      Post-Tender Negotiation Response Checking


Step 1: A draft of negotiation response checking e-Form is auto-generated under “Drafts”
section upon negotiation response by supplier.





Step 2: Download the document submitted by supplier.

Step 3: Fill in a remark and identify the compliance result.

Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.






Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.
