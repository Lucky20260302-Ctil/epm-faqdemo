Following section 6.3.2.2.1, buyer receives an email notifcation for completion on technical
opening endorsement.

**Step 1:** Buyer creates a new e-Form for starting a technical evaluation under “New Requests”
section, selecting the relevant RFQ / Tender to proceed.

**Step 2:** Input the target completion date.

**Step 3:** Select the technical assessor for further proceeding.

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 6:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 7:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

> **Email:** An email notification for submitting individual scoring is sent to the selected assessor(s) after
submission.

Assessor is required to complete the DOI before accessing the technical folder, refer to section

### 6.2.1 for DOI.

**Step 1:** A drafted e-Form of submitting individual scoring is auto-generated for assessor(s) in
“Drafts” section after technical evaluation be lanuched by Buyer.

**Step 2:** Click to download the technical proposal(s) for review.

**Step 3:** Submit technical scoring by “Edit Score”, the overall passing score is be displayed for
reference.

The passing score for individual item is displayed within the scoring sheet.

Remark is required to fill in if any items below the passing score.

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

> **Email:** An email notification for compliance verification is sent to Buyer after submission.

Buyer is required to conduct an assessment score checking upon submission from assessor(s)

> **Email:** with an email notification.

**Step 1:** A drafted e-Form of Technical Assessment Score Checking is auto-generated for Buyer
under “Drafts” section.

**Step 2:** Review the scoring sheet details.

**Step 3:** Select “Compliance” for further proceeding, or;

Return to assessor(s) for revising with reason(s) of such return.

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

Technical and/or Fee Clarifications may be required at different stages for RFQ / Tender and
Buyer can issue clarifications to individual supplier(s) upon obtaining appropriate approval.

**Step 1:** Buyer creates a new e-Form for clarification under “New Requests” section, selecting
the relevant RFQ / Tender to proceed.

**Step 2:** Type of clarification is identified by the system according to stages of technical or fee
assessment.

Specify the respond deadline, upload clarification document and supplement details .

**Step 3:** Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission, and

> **Email:** clarification is issued to the selected supplier automatically with email notifications upon
approval completion.

**Step 1:** A draft of clarification response checking e-Form is auto-generated under “Drafts”
section upon negotiation response by supplier.

**Step 2:** Download the document submitted by supplier.

**Step 3:** Fill in a remark and identify the compliance result.

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

**Step 1:** A drafted e-Form for technical assessment result endorsement is auto-generated for
Buyer after all the technical scoring are submitted with compliance.

**Step 2:** Review the complete technical assessment result.

**Step 3:** Endorse for the technical assessment result.

Restart of technical evaluation is required if endorsement is rejected.

**Step 4:** Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

**Step 5:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 6:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 7:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the E-form”.

An email for endorsement request is sent to the selected approver after submission.
