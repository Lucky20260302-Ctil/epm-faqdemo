6.3.4  Restart of Technical Evaluation


Upon completion of technical assessment, the finance users can submit an approval request
regarding restart of the technical evaluation before opening team members initiating the fee
opening.

Step 1: Buyer creates a new e-Form for restart of technical evaluation under “New Requests”
section, selecting the relevant RFQ / Tender to proceed.






Step 2: Input details negotiation, including:

   •  Target Completion Date
   •  Reason(s) for Restart
   •  Upload Supporting Document





Step 3: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.
