6.1 Pre-Tender Stage

    6.1.1  Procurement Initiation
This process initiates the procurement exercise and sets the foundation for Tender or RFQ
issuance.

Step 1: The Requestor creates Procurement Initiation e-Form under “New Requests” section.






Step 2: Input the basic procurement strategy information.

The columns of “Estimated Values of the Contract” (non-editable) and “Buyer In-Charge” are
auto-filleda fter purchase requisition items are added below.





If Pre-Qualification was completed previously, a reminder will prompt to attach related
information.





Step 3: Select the sourcing method, Tender / RFQ approach and submission method (only
online submission is available in Phase 1 launch).



Step 4: Select the declaration method for conflict of interest which is defaulted as declaration
through the system.





Offline declarations are required to upload the supporting document(s).







Step 5: Specify the procurement category.

Click   button to expand the category details, click  button to select the whole category.

One (1) primary category must be selected via checkbox.





Step 6: Click      to select approved PR for the current procurement initiation.

 Click   to expand the PR details, only unlocked items can be chosen, suggested buyer is auto-
filled into the “Procurement Officer In-Charge” above and suggested supplier is auto-filled
under the supplier list with system message reminder.






Step 7: Select the expected contract period.






Step 8: Update the pricing schedule setting (only submission as attachment or system e-Form is
available for Phase 1 launch).





For submitting pricing schedule as attachment, the Requestor can update the description,
quantity and unit price of selected PR items in the requisition item list.

Total estimated amount is calculated and updated in the above section after updates.






For system pricing schedule e-Form submission, the Requestor needs to set up the item groups
for supplier input while the PR items in the requisition item list are read-only for reference.

Click     to add PR items for each group. Item description (editable), quantity (editable), UOM
and estimated unit price will auto fill in after selection of PR items.

The Requestor can decide the necessity for supplier response, and predefine the items
included in Tender / RFQ sum for award.






Total estimated amount is calculated and updated in the above section after updates.

Step 9: Fill in the procurement information, procurement title is defaulted as procurement
initiation subject and is editable.







Reminder pop if the RFQ / Tender period is less than the minimum requirement, while
exception approval is triggered during submission of Tender / RFQ issuance (Refer to section
6.1.3 for details).





The closing date for Tender / RFQ that will open in Hong Kong can be auto-postponed under
Hong Kong extreme weather conditions.






Step 10: Identify the assessment panel setting. Technical assessment panel and price assessor
(Allow update by e-Form, refer to section 6.1.4 for assignment details) are required to fill in.

Click   button to add the assessment panel, one column for one assessor.

Input name for filtering user or click    to search from the list.

Three (3) minimum of technical assessors (requiring at least two (2) assessors from the same
of purchasing department) and two (2) minimum of price assessors , buyer cannot be either
one of the assessors or approvers, the remarks field is mandatory and exception approval is
required if technical assessor with insufficient grade is selected.





Step 11: Add shortlisting criteria and supplier for invitation.

Click      to search supplier from list and add (Refer to section 5.2 for new supplier invitation
and section 5.3 for FMS supplier activation)

Suggested suppliers are brought from the selected PR available to add to the list.

Random selection of supplier is available to random draw supplier with the selected
procurement category, and remarks is required if the random supplier is not chosen.

Exception approval is required for insufficient bidders / tenderers.






Remarks are mandatory to input for the supplier who do not have the required minimum
performance score.

Step 12: Define the assessment method. Input remarks for “Not Applicable”, or;







Set up the weighting for assessment. The total weighting must equal to 100 and the technical
passing score is mandatory to set which should not be over the marks input for “Technical
Weighting”.

Justification is required when “Technical Weighting” is set above 70.

(Additional round of technical evaluation is not available for Phase 1 launch.)

Step 13: Add assessment criteria and weighting which should be equal to marks set in
“Technical Weighting” in total.

Passing mark can be set for individual criteria.
If no passing mark is set for any individual assessment criterion, the average total technical score
of each supplier determines the pass / disqualified.
If passing mark is set for any individual assessment criterion, tenderer / bidder is regarded as
technical disqualified when tenderer/ bidder failed in any individual criteria even if the total
technical score is passed.





Step 14: Fill in other information if needed.






Step 15: Upload the Tender / RFQ documents.





Step 16: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details
(Refer to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Reason(s) for exception approval must be stated.





Step 17: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.



Step 18: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 19: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the E-form”.

An email for approval request is sent to selected approver after submission.
