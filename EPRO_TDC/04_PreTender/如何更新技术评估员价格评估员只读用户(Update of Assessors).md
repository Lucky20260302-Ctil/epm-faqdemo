6.1.4  Update of Technical Assessor / Price Assessor / View-only User


Buyer can update the technical assessor, price assessor and view-only user for current RFQ /
Tender by submitting new approval request.

Step 1: Select the relevant e-Form for update under “New Requests” section.





Step 2: Input the RFQ / Tender or procurement initiation reference, or select from the list by
clicking     , click “Create” to proceed.






Step 3: Update the selected users.

Step 4: Select the approval route from dropdown menu, and specify the approval details (Refer
to section 4.2.2.2 for approval settings).

Step 5: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.





Step 6: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 7: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An email for approval request is sent to the selected approver after submission.
