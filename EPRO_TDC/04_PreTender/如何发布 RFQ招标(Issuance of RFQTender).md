6.1.3  Issuance of RFQ / Tender


Draft of Issuance of RFQ / Tender is auto-generated in the buyer’s account upon approval of
Tender Setting.

Step 1: Buyer selects the relevant e-Form under “Drafts” section.





Step 2: Review the issue and closing date time, exception approval is triggered at this e-Form
for insufficient RFQ / Tendering period.






Step 3: No approval route is required if no exception approval is triggered, click “Review &
Validate” to proceed.





Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the e-Form”.

An invitation email for RFQ / Tender is sent to the selected supplier after submission.
