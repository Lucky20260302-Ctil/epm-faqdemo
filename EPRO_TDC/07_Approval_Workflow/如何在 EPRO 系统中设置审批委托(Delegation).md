The Platform allows internal user to designate a proxy to perform specific operations or
approvals within a defined timeframe, following predefined rules. Validation Check: Delegation cannot be assigned to a user with a lower staff grade.

**Step 1:** Go to “Approval Delegation”

**Step 2:** Select the delegated-to-person and the effective period of delegation.

**Step 3:** Specify the type of approval requests to be rounted, including pre-tender stage, tender
stage, post tender stage and supplier-management-related.

**Step 4:** Fill in the “Remarks” field.

**Step 5:** Attach supporting document (optional) and click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 6:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 7:** Review and confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting
the E-form”.

After submission, delegation becomes effective during the specific period. Email notifications
are sent to both delegator and delegated-to-person.

The delegated-to-person can access relevant approval requests under “Delegated Approvals” in

> **Email:** the “Pending Approvals” page after receiving an email notification.

Once approved, the action is recorded under e-Form approval history.
