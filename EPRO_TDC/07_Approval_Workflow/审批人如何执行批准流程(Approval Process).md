Approvers can approve an e-Form if all the details are confirmed without conflict of interest
(Refer to 5.3.3 for detailed steps of undeclared conflict of interest).

**Step 1:** Click “Continue to Approval” to begin the review process.

**Step 2:** Declare conflict of interest.

The system defaults the declaration as “No” conflict of interest.

If a potential conflict of interest in declared in the HR system and approved, the Approver is
required to upload the supporting document under the “Action” section.

**Step 3:** To approve the request, click “Approve”. Optionally, Approver can supplement for
remarks or attach supporting document for reference. And Approver clicks “Submit Approval”
to proceed.

> **Email:** Requestor receives email notification upon approval complete, and approver can review the
approval record "My Task" > "Completed Task" > “Approvals”
