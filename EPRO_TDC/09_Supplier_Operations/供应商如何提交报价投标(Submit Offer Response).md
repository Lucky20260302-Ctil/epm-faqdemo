6.1.6   Offer Response


6.1.6.1  Submit Offer


A draft of the Offer Response is auto-generated for suppliers.

Steps to Submit Offer Response:

Step 1: Log in to EPRO.

Step 2: Go to “Drafts” and click to review the RFQ / Tender document and submit response .





Step 3: Download and review the RFQ / Tender documents and any Addendum uploaded by
HKTDC.






6.1.6.1.1 Submission by Proposal:


Step 4: Upload the proposal to the Technical and Price folders.

Step 5: Enter the total quote for the current submission.






Step 6: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

Step 7: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 8: Confirm the terms and conditions. OTP validation is required for offer-submission-
related responses .





Step 9: Enter the OTP and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.





Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission.







6.1.6.1.2 Submission by Pricing Schedule e-Form:


Step 4: Fill in the items quote details, and supplement with remarks (optional).





Step 5: Upload the proposal to the Technical and Price folders.





Step 6: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

Step 7: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 8: Confirm the terms and conditions. OTP validation is required for offer-submission-
related responses .





Step 9: Enter the OTP and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.





Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission.
