6.1.6.2  Submit No Offer


Supplier may decline to submit an offer before the RFQ / Tender closing date.

Steps to Submit No Offer

Step 1: Log in to EPRO.

Step 2: Go to “New Request” and select Decline Offer , and specify the relevant RFQ / Tender by

entering the reference numbder or selecting from the list by       .






Step 3: Click to confirm that you do not proceed with the offer response.

Step 4: Select the decline reason from the dropdown list.





Step 5: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

Step 6: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 7: Confirm the terms and conditions. OTP validation is required for offer-submission-
related responses.






Step 8: Enter the OTP and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.





Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission.
