A draft of the Non-Disclosure Agreement Submission is auto-generated for the supplier if
required before accessing RFQ / Tender information.

**Step 1:** Log in to EPRO.

**Step 2:** Click to review and submit NDA / Confidentiality Undertaking under “Drafts”.

**Step 3:** Download and review the NDA / Confidentiality Undertaking uploaded by HKTDC.

**Step 4:** Sign and returned by to upload the signed document.

**Step 5:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 6:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 7:** Confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.

> **Email:** Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission and is notified with a
response checking result from HKTDC.
