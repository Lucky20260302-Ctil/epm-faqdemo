3 System Login


A unique system login ID will be granted to Supplier in account activation email.






3.1 Account Activation


Step 1: Log in to EPRO from the activation link in the invitation email.

Step 2: Input your email address.

Step 3: Click “Next” to further proceed.






Step 3: Click the “Email Address” and receive One-Time Passcode (OTP) from email with
successful system message, input for account validation.






Step 4: Set up password for login account by following the password policy.






Step 5: Set up successfully and re-login by user ID and password.
