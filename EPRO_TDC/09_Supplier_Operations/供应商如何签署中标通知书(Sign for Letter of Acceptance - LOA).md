6.2.2  Sign for Letter of Acceptance (LOA)


Supplier will receive email notification on RFQ / Tender Award, providing a draft of LOA
documents for review and return a signed copy.

Step 1: Log in to EPRO.

Step 2: Go to “Drafts” and click to review the award notification and submit your response.





Step 3: Download and review the LOA documents.

Step 4: Attach signed copies and supplement with remarks (optional).






Step 5: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

Step 6: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 7: Confirm the terms and conditions. OTP validation is required for offer-submission-
related responses.





Step 8: Enter the OTP and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.






Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission as below.
