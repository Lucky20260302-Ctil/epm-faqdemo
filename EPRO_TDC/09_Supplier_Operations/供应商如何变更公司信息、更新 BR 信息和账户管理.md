5.2 Change Supplier Information


Potential Suppliers: Not allowed to update company information and updates should be
managed exclusively by TDC.

Registered / Approved Suppliers: Can update supplier profile at anytime, except in “Locked”
status pending approval from HKTDC for previous registration or supplier’s updates.

Step 1: Log in to EPRO.

Step 2: Click “Update Supplier Info by External” under “New Requests”.





Step 3: Update supplier information based on existing details, as described in 4.1. All fields are
editable, except company name limited to HKTDC users’ update.

Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.






Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission.






5.3 Update BR Information


Suppliers can update BR information anytime, except in “Locked” status pending approval from
HKTDC for previous updates.

Step 1: Log in to EPRO.

Step 2: Click “Update Business Registration Information” under “New Requests”.





Step 3: Update BR information based on existing information.





Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.


Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission.





   5.4 Master / Sub Account Update


EPRO provides a master account and up to five (5) sub-accounts for each supplier, enabling
participation in RFQ / Tender response.

The master account is generated when a pre-registration or full registration invitation is issued
to the supplier with specific supplier login ID. The first 11 digits in login ID representing the
unique supplier code while the last digit of 0 to 5 representing the account number of supplier
(0 = master account login, 1 – 5 = sub-accounts login).

Only the master account can assign sub-accounts with user roles of Company Admin and
Tender Admin. The master account and sub-accounts with the Company Admin role can update
company information, including contact details for both master account and sub-accounts.

Step 1: Log in to EPRO.

Step 2: Click “Master / Sub Account Update” under “New Requests”.







Step 3: Update the contact information for master and sub-accounts.





Step 4: Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

Step 5: Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

Step 6: Confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.

Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission.
