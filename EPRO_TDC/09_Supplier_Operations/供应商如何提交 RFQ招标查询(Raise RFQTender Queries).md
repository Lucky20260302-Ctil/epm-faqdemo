Supplier can submit queries before a specific period (typically four (4) hours) before the RFQ /
Tender closing time.

**Step 1:** Log in to EPRO.

**Step 2:** Raise an RFQ / Tender query under “New Request”, and specify the relevant RFQ /

Tender by inputting the reference numbder or selecting from the list by .

**Step 3:** Attach query documents and provide supplementary details. Please

> **Note:** note that any fee
information is included in the query may result in disqualification from this RFQ / Tender
without prior notification.

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.

> **Email:** Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission. Supplier is notified if
a query response is issued by HKTDC and the relevant details are auto-generated under
“Completed Tasks” for review.
