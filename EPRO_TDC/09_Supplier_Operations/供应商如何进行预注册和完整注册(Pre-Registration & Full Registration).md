5 Supplier Management

Suppliers are invited to submit for either pre-registration or full registration process in EPRO.
Suppliers are categorized into the following statuses during the registration process for RFQ &
Tender activities: Status Description Purpose
 Unregistered Received pre-registration invitation but not Invitation for upcoming RFQ / Tender yet submitted the registration form. participation.
 Registering Submitted the pre-registration form and Requires HKTDC’s responses to pending for HKTDC verification. validate submitted registration form.
 Potential Pre-registration approved by HKTDC. Supplier with approved pre- registration are qualified for RFQ / Tender invitation and participation in RFQ / Tender activities.
 Registered Supplier who completes the full registration Supplier is required to activate full and awarded for RFQ. registration supplier account and submit full registration form to HKTDC before awarding a RFQ.
 Approved Supplier who completes the full registration Supplier is required to activate full and awarded for Tender. registration supplier account and submit full registration form for HKTDC before awarding a Tender.

## 5.1 Supplier Pre-Registration & Full Registration

Supplier should access EPRO for account activation and submit the registration form upon
receiving an email invitation by HKTDC.

Step 1 Activation-: Activate the supplier account by following the detailed steps from section
3.1.

Step 2 Draft Form-: A draft of (either pre or full) registration form is auto-generated under
“Drafts”. Click to select the relevant draft to complete.

For Pre-Registration: Supplier is unable to participate in RFQ / Tender exercises, or create
other e-Form under “New Requests” if the pre-registration is not yet submitted and approved
by TDC.

Step 3 Contact Information: Fill in the contact information, declare conflicts of interest and
submit the registration documents.

Step 4 Supporting Documents: Upload additional supporting documents if required.

For Full Registration: Supplier are invited for the supplier full registration before RFQ / Tender
award to require supplier to supplement additional company information, or invited FMS
supplier for account activation in EPRO.

Step 3 Form Sections: Fill in the information from section 1 to 10.

Step 4 Supporting Documents: Upload supporting document for reference (optional).

**Step 5:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 6:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 7:** Confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.

> **Email:** Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission and is notified with a
response checking result from HKTDC.
