A draft of the Briefing / Site Visit is auto-generated for the supplier to complete.Suppliers is not
allowed to submit an offer if registration is mandotory.

**Step 1:** Log in to EPRO.

**Step 2:** Click to review Briefing / Site Visit under “Drafts”.

**Step 3:** Supplier can either download, fill in and return the registration form with attachment,
or;

Fill in the registration e-Form by for adding attendee contact information.

**Step 4:** Click “Review & Validate” to proceed.

**Step 5:** Click “Confirm & Submit” after review.

**Step 6:** Confirm the terms and conditions and click “Yes, proceed with submitting the e-Form”.

> **Email:** Supplier receives an email acknowledgement upon successful submission and is notified with
an attendance confirmation result from HKTDC.
