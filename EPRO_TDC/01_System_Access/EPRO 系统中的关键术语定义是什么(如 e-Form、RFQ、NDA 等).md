2. Definition of Terms

 Terms                        Definition
 Approval Delegation        The process allowing an internal user to assign another user
                                as a proxy to perform specific approval actions within a
                               designated period, subject to grade-based validation
                                     restrictions.
 Azure AD                      Microsoft Azure Active Directory, used for authenticating
                                  internal users' system login via single sign-on.
 Co-editor                    Users who are granted permission to review, edit and submit
                                the e-Form, with the same level of access as the Initiator/
                                Requestor.
 Dashboard                  User interface in the system displaying notifications, tasks,
                           and key functional controls for managing e-Forms and
                                 approvals.
 Draft                    An e-Form status indicating the form is being completed or
                              saved but not yet submitted.
 e-Form                  An electronic form used for initiating and managing
                             procurement and approval workflows within the system.
 FMS Only Supplier         A supplier profile existing only in the Financial Management
                               System, eligible to participate in RFQ/Tender upon
                                    activation.
 Initiator                   The User who creates the e-Form and draft will be save
                            under “My Task – Drafts”.
 Internal Users                Users operating within the organization’s network, primarily
                                accessing the system via office workstations.
 NDA                   A document assuring confidentiality of sensitive information
 (Non-Disclosure              provided during tender or procurement stages.
 Agreement)
 OTP                   A temporary password sent to authorized users to verify
 (One-Time Password)          identity during sensitive operations such as tender box
                                 unlocking.
 Pre-Registration            The initial stage of supplier registration, allowing limited
                                access ahead of full supplier registration.
 Procurement Initiation      The starting phase of a procurement exercise where the
                             procurement strategy and key details are defined and
                                submitted.



Requestor                 The User who requests for the e-Form and draft will be save
                            under “My Task – Other Pending Actions”.
 RFQ                    A formal request inviting suppliers to submit pricing
 (Request for Quotation)       proposals for specified goods or services.
 Approval Route           A multi-level approval workflow within the e-Form system
                                 involving multiple approvers with specific roles.
 Session Timeout             Automatic termination of user sessions after a preset
                                    inactivity duration, e.g., after 1 hour of inactivity for internal
                                   users.
 Supplier Status                  Classification of suppliers such as Unregistered, Registering,
                                    Potential, Registered, Approved, FMS Only, or Terminated,
                                 defining their participation rights.
 Tender                 A structured procurement process comprising submission,
                                  evaluation, and award phases.
 Tender Opening            The formal event and associated e-Form processes for
                             opening submitted tender proposals.
 Technical Assessor         A designated individual responsible for evaluating the
                                  technical components of supplier submissions.
 View-only User           A user with read-only access to specific e-Forms, without
                              permission to edit or submit.
