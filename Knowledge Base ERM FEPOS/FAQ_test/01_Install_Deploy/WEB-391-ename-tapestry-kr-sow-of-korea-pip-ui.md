## 問題描述

**A.    **<u>**Customer Inquiry and Update**</u>

-  **“Surname”, “First name”, “Email Address”, “Home Phone” **and** “Mobile Phone” **fields on the member inquiry result list will be masked based on the corresponding configuration settings.

- The customer detail page is not required to apply masking after selecting the specific customer.

- Complete member information without masking can only be viewed by privileged users who have the right to modify member information.
