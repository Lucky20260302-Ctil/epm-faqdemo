## 需求描述

- **3.6** Submission of Quotations / Tenders
    - **3.6.6** Query and Addendum (g) Subject Officers shall be allowed to amend the tender closing to a later date and/or time with mandatory input of justifications and appropriate approvals obtained on the Platform. Notification should be sent to all invited suppliers for such change. An email notification shall also be sent to responsible RFQ / Tender Opening Team for change of closing date and/or time upon approval and issuance of related addenda.
## 驗收標準

The e-Form for submitting an approval request for extension closing date and time can be used before invitation closing date and time of the RFQ / Tender:
Depending on the configuration set by the Procurement Officers, as outlined in FR-RTR-023, the Platform will automatically postpone the final closing date in the event of a delay in approval.
The following report is available for internal users with access control to track the status of Extension Closing Date and Time Requests:
RFQ / Tender Data View: This report indicates whether any Extension Closing Date and Time Requests have been  for the RFQ / Tender. If they have, it lists them along with submission dates and times, as well as approval dates and times.
The Platform can generate standard notification emails for internal users and external suppliers based on specific triggers:
Here are the available options and features for the e-Form:
The preparer drafts the e-Form. Drafts can be saved automatically to prevent data loss in case of a session timeout.
The e-Form can be saved in PDF and Excel formats.
PDF version: for attaching to emails for review and audit trails.
Excel version: Helps the preparer in creating documents for off-system / external processes.
Change history: The Platform automatically logs each field, capturing the date and time, the changer's details, the field modified, and old/new values.
Amendment history: The preparer manually fills in the summary of changes with each save.
Action History captures every e-Form status change, specifying the date, time, initiator, and new status.
A weekly reminder email is sent to the preparer who have pending tasks to complete.
