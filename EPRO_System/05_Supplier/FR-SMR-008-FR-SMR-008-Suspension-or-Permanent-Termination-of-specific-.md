## 需求描述

3.39	F&A Users shall also be allowed to deactivate the status of a supplier upon obtaining the necessary approvals.
3.51	All related supplier information, including new registrations, amendments and deactivations shall be promptly updated on the Platform upon review and approval of F&A Users and/or appropriate approvers.
3.52	The Platform shall provide support for the suspension or permanent termination of specific supplier(s) under special circumstances. The following features should be included at a minimum:
3.52.1	The ability to set the duration of suspension or permanent termination for each case.
3.52.2	The capability to upload relevant documents to support the termination or suspension.
3.52.3	An email notification to the designated F&A Users one month prior to the end of suspension period.
3.52.4	An approval flow for changing supplier type from “Normal” to “Suspended” supplier type, and vice versa.
