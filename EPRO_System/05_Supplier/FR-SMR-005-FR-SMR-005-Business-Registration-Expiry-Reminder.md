## 需求描述

3.40	The input of expiry date of the business registration certificate shall be one of the mandatory fields for supplier registration. It should be noted that the expiry date may not be applicable for business registration certificates of certain countries. The Contractor shall advise a mechanism to handle this difference. If the expiry date is applicable for business registration certificates, the Contractor shall configure / customise the Platform with the following reminder features:
3.40.1	To send a reminder to F&A User(s) stating all the suppliers of selected supplier type(s) whose business registration certificate will expire in the following month; and/or
3.40.2	To send a business registration submission request to relevant suppliers with business registration certificate expiring in the following month.

## 驗收標準

For potential, registered and approved suppliers, the Platform configured the expiry date of the business registration certificate as a conditional field for supplier registration and supplier information update. If a supplier selects the Registered Country as "Hong Kong", the expiry date of the business registration certificate becomes mandatory; otherwise, it remains optional.
