## 需求描述

    - **3.1.9** The Platform shall obtain the employee hierarchy with information including internal grade, department, and reporting line to support the approval flow by integrating with relevant internal system.
## 驗收標準

The Platform support appropriate approvals at a certain grade, use the API provided by HKTDC to collect details such as internal grade, department, and reporting line when the requestor prepares the approval request.
This interface requirement will be documented and reviewed during the subsequent SA&D stage.
