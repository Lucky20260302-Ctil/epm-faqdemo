## 需求描述

    - **3.1.30** The Tenderers participating in this tender shall demonstrate their ability to develop a Platform that can accommodate both offline and online submissions for quotations or tenders simultaneously.
## 驗收標準

The Platform support online submission, offline submission, and hybrid mode. The submission mode should be specified in the Procurement Strategy e-Form before creating an RFQ/Tender exercise.
If the online submission mode is selected, External Suppliers must submit quotations or tenders online. No offline submissions are permitted.
If offline submission or hybrid mode is selected, External Suppliers can submit quotations or tenders offline. Additionally, the Tender Opening Administrative Team can upload scan-copies of technical and fee proposals into the Platform and finalize the RFQ/Tender Opening Record during the RFQ/Tender Opening stage.
Outlined below are specific scenarios with their corresponding exception handling:
For NDA/confidential undertaking, if specified and enabled in the RFQ/Tender exercise, bidder/tenderer have the option to submit a scan copy of the signed NDA/confidential undertaking either online or offline. The subject officer can release the RFQ/Tender documents after a compliance check.
For RFQ/Tender briefing/site visit, if specified and enabled in the RFQ/Tender exercise, bidder/tenderer can submit a registration form for the briefing/site visit either online or offline. Should any bidder/tenderer fail to attend the RFQ/Tender briefing, the subject officer can exclude bidder/tenderer from the Bidder/Tenderer List before the RFQ/Tender closes.
For RFQ/Tender queries, if the bidder/tenderer raises queries offline, the subject officer can respond to RFQ/Tender queries and upload the scan-copies of queries received as internal supporting documents.
The Use Case will be documented in the RFQ and Tender section of the e-Procurement Requirements.
