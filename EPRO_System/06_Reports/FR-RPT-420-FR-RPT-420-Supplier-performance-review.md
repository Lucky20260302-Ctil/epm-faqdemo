## 需求描述

D.	Generation of Reports / Documents
- **3.56** Managerial and statistical reports to be generated by the Platform (with different filtering criteria e.g. period, department and service type, etc.) shall include but not limited to the following: (e) Supplier performance review
## 驗收標準

The Platform supports the following user roles to export the details in Excel:
Finance users
The Platform exports the following details in Excel:
Summary: This worksheet lists the number of suppliers and their percentage % based on their performance scores, categorized by ranges (0 and below 6, 6 and below 7, 7 and below 9, and 9-10), categorized by procurement category and by month over a period of three years.
Raw Data: This worksheet provides the raw data used to create the Summary worksheet.
Note: The detailed design will be provided in a later SA&D stage.
