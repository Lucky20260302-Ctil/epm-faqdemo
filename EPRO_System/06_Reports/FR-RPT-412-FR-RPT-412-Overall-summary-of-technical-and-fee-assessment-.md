## 需求描述

D.	Generation of Reports / Documents
3.55.	Summaries and documents to be generated for the features and functions related to different procurement procedures shall include but not limited to the following:
(l) Overall summary of technical and fee assessment with quotation summary and calculated combine score (for Two Envelope Approach)

## 驗收標準

The Platform support feature to export the Overall Assessment Summary in PDF, as outlined in FR-RTR-208.
This report provides detailed information on:
Bidders/tenderers along with their supplier names.
The technical marks given by each panel member for each bidder/tenderer.
The average technical scores of each bidder/tenderer.
The total quoted amount for each technically passed bidder/tenderer.
The weighted price score of each technically passed bidder/tenderer.
The combined score of each technically passed bidder/tenderer
