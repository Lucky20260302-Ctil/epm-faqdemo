## 需求描述

D.	Generation of Reports / Documents
3.56. Managerial and statistical reports to be generated by the Platform (with different filtering criteria e.g. period, department and service type, etc.) shall include but not limited to the following:
(g) Spending / Purchase or award amount

## 驗收標準

The Platform supports the following user roles to export the details in Excel:
Finance users
The Platform exports the following details in Excel:
Summary: This worksheet lists the total budget/estimated amount and the total award amount, categorized by sourcing category, by user group and by month over a period of three year.
Raw Data: This worksheet provides the raw data used to create the Summary worksheet.
Note: The detailed design will be provided in a later SA&D stage.
