## 需求描述

D.	Generation of Reports / Documents
3.55.	Summaries and documents to be generated for the features and functions related to different procurement procedures shall include but not limited to the following:
(e) Pricing schedules

## 驗收標準

The Platform allows bidders/tenderers to export the Pricing Schedule e-Form to an Excel file, as outlined in FR-RTR-012.
After filling out the Excel file, bidders/tenderers must import it back into the Platform and perform online validation. If the validation is successful, bidders/tenderers can download the Pricing Schedule in PDF format and submit the finalized Pricing Schedule to the E-Tender Box along with their Technical Proposal and/or Fee Proposal.
