# BE-693: Record of additional price displayed as cleared & cannot check back added addictional on sales

## 問題描述

Env: [https://172.16.138.101/chainstoreplus/](https://172.16.138.101/chainstoreplus/)

Location: On Sale Price Maintenance - Page Mode (MF2003)

Reproduce step:

1. To On Sale Price Maintenance - Page Mode

2. Click on one of the records

3. Click Edit additional on sales price

4. Add some additional price

5. Click "X" and select Save

6. Click back the record again

7. Click Edit additional price again

Existing result:

Record of additional price displayed as cleared & cannot check back added addictional on sales price group (Ref: ChainStorePlus _ 99 - ERM Company 99 - Google Chrome 2022-11-11 10-45-27)
