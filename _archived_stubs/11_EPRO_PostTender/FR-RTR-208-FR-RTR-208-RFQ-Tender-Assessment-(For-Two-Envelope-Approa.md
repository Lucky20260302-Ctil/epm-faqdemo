# FR-RTR-208: FR-RTR-208: RFQ / Tender Assessment (For Two Envelope Approach)

## 需求描述

Requirement ID:	FR-RTR-208	Requirement Type:	RFQ and Tender	Requirements
Parent Requirement #:
None
Description:
3.7.	RFQ / Tender Assessment
3.7.2	Two Envelope Approach
(a) Each technical assessment panel member (assigned as per Section 3.4.3(b)(iv)) shall be required to submit declaration of confidentiality and conflict of interest to the invited suppliers. The composition of assessment panel may be changed based on the results of declaration.
(b) After making the declaration, assessment panel members shall be able to access the related documents for technical assessment including:
(i) Individual Technical Scoring Sheets (generated by the Platform with information inputted by the Subject Officer as per Section 3.4.3(b)(iv));
(ii) All received technical proposals (uploaded by the Subject Officer for offline submission or by invited suppliers for online submission on the Platform); and
(iii) Other relevant documents (uploaded by the Subject Officer or generated by the Platform)
(c) The Platform shall validate the entries of assessment panel members before allowing submission. It shall be mandatory to provide comments for failing marks in the total score, criteria and/or sub-criteria.
(d) The Platform shall allow flexibility for the Subject Officer to upload assessment panel member’s Individual Technical Scoring Sheet and input the marks into the Platform for technical assessment done off-system.
(e) A notification email with hyperlink to the Platform shall be sent to all assessment panel members when the Subject Officer confirms that all assessment documents are ready for conducting technical assessment.
(f) Upon submission of Individual Technical Scoring Sheet by all panel members, a notification email shall be sent to the responsible Subject Officer. The Platform shall also allow the responsible Subject Officer to view the submission status of each assessment panel member (i.e. whether they have completed the submission or not).
(g) The Platform shall calculate the average mark for each individual assessment criterion, sub-criterion and the total technical score for each supplier.
(h) Only the fee proposal of a supplier who achieves a passing score on the total technical score and passes all required criteria/sub-criteria (if applicable) would be opened. Dual / multiple password mechanisms shall also be applied for the opening of fee proposals.
(i) The Platform shall allow Subject Officer to upload fee proposals submitted off-system and input fee information for calculation of weighted fee score.
(j) The Platform shall calculate the technical score, weighted price score and combined score based on the relative weightings assigned to the technical and price assessments for each specific RFQ / tender. A formula will be provided for this calculation.
(k) The Platform shall generate assessment summaries in the prescribed layout, including supplier names, technical marks given by each panel member, average technical scores, total quoted amount, weighted price score and combined score.
(l) If there are fee clarifications that results in changes to the fees, a second version of the assessment summary shall be generated. Justifications are required for any modifications to fees. All versions of the assessment summary shall be maintained on the Platform with clear indication for version control.
(m) Once the respective assessment is completed, assessment panel members shall not be allowed to retrieve, access or download the documents related to the assessment, including technical proposals.

Acceptance / Fit Criteria:
Each member of the technical assessment panel, including Technical Assessors and Observers, is assigned via the Procurement Strategy e-Form, as specified in FR-RTR-001. They are required to submit their Declaration of Interest (DOI) online and based on the handling as outlined in FR-RTR-013. The composition of the assessment panel may be modified depending on the outcomes of the DOI submissions.
This process depends on the configuration of the RFQ / Tender exercise, as outlined in FR-RTR-028. The procedures required for conducting the RFQ / Tender Evaluation online include:
Step 1 – Start the RFQ / Tender Evaluation
Step 2 – Submit Technical Scores for the Semi-final Round with Compliance Verification
– Submit Technical Scores for the Final Round with Compliance Verification
– Request for RFQ / Tender Opening for Fee Proposals
– Submit Request for Modification of Fees and/or Total Amounts
– Endorsement for Overall Assessment Summary and Completion of the RFQ / Tender Evaluation
The procedures required for conducting the RFQ / Tender Evaluation offline include:
Step 1 – Start the RFQ / Tender Evaluation
Step 2 – Submit Technical Scores for the Final Round
Step 4 – Request for RFQ / Tender Opening for Fee Proposals
Step 5 – Submit Request for Modification of Fees and/or Total Amounts
Step 6 – Endorsement for Overall Assessment Summary and Completion of the RFQ / Tender Evaluation
The e-Forms used during RFQ / Tender Evaluation:
The RFQ / Tender exercise may require e-Form for management and control, which can be used during RFQ / Tender Evaluation:
Here are the available options and features for the e-Form:
The preparer drafts the e-Form. Drafts can be saved automatically to prevent data loss in case of a session timeout.
The e-Form can be saved in PDF and Excel formats.
PDF version: for attaching to emails for review and audit trails.
Excel version: Helps the preparer in creating documents for off-system / external processes.
The e-Form can be submitted for approval by the requestor designated by the preparer. If the requestor is designated, the e-Form can be reviewed, edited, and submitted for approval. Following submission, email notifications are sent to individuals based on the approval route, considering parallel or sequential modes.
Approvers (Reviewers, endorsers, and final approvers) can take actions such as approve, reject, return with feedback(s) or withdraw, provided that specific conditions are met.
The requestor is allowed to revoke approval at any time, enabling them to cancel the approval request before the final approver grants approval.
In cases where approval requests involve budget virement issues, the requestor can decide whether to inform the controlling officer of the budget division/department after approval or seek their endorsement within the approval route before final approval.
If an approval request requires the Tender Board Chairman's endorsement before issuing Tender Invitations, Addendums, Extending the closing date/time, or terminating the tender, the requestor can decide whether to inform the Tender Board Chairman after approval or seek their endorsement within the approval route before final approval.
Change history: The Platform automatically logs each field, capturing the date and time, the changer's details, the field modified, and old/new values.
Amendment history: The preparer manually fills in the summary of changes with each save.
Action History captures every e-Form status change, specifying the date, time, initiator, and new status.
Approval history tracks overall approval status changes, including the date, time, and last approver. It also logs individual actions with the date, time, person's name, action taken (e.g., approve, reject, withdraw), reasons or remarks, and attachments if necessary.
A weekly reminder email is sent to the requestor and current approvers who are pending approval based on the approval route.
The Platform should provide Approval Workflow feature, as outlined in FR-GR-012.
