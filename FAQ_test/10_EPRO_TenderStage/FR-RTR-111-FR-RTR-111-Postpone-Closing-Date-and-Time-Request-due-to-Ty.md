# FR-RTR-111: FR-RTR-111: Postpone Closing Date and Time Request due to Typhoon Signal No. 8 or above or Black Rainstorm Warning

## 需求描述

Requirement ID:	FR-RTR-111	Requirement Type:	RFQ and Tender	Requirements
Parent Requirement #:
None
Description:
This requirement addresses the need for managing requests to postpone the closing date and time due to typhoon signal no. 8 or above or a black rainstorm warning.
Rationale:
None
Acceptance / Fit Criteria:
The e-Form for submitting a request to postpone closing date and time can be used before invitation closing date and time of the RFQ / Tender:
The following report is available for internal users with access control to track the status of Postpone Closing Date and Time Requests:
RFQ / Tender Data View: This report indicates whether any Postpone Closing Date and Time Requests have been made for the RFQ / Tender. If they have, it lists them along with submission dates and times.
The Platform can generate standard notification emails for internal users based on specific triggers:
Note: Email notification to bidders/tenderers is not provided, as the procedures are already outlined in the Instruction to Bidders/Tenderers.
Here are the available options and features for the e-Form:
The preparer drafts the e-Form. Drafts can be saved automatically to prevent data loss in case of a session timeout.
The e-Form can be saved in PDF and Excel formats.
PDF version: for attaching to emails for review and audit trails.
Excel version: Helps the preparer in creating documents for off-system / external processes.
Change history: The Platform automatically logs each field, capturing the date and time, the changer's details, the field modified, and old/new values.
Amendment history: The preparer manually fills in the summary of changes with each save.
Action History captures every e-Form status change, specifying the date, time, initiator, and new status.
A weekly reminder email is sent to the the preparer who have pending tasks to complete.
