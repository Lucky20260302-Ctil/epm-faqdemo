Description:
3.8.	Approval of Award Recommendation
3.8.1.	Preparation of Award Recommendation
(b) Subject Officers shall submit the above entries of the award recommendation for appropriate approvers, based on the prescribed threshold (total contract price of a single RFQ / tender shall be counted, regardless of the awarding of multiple suppliers), which may involve multiple stages. Attachment(s)shall be included for approval, depending on RFQ / tender approach.
(c) A Tender Board, typically comprised of 3-5 members, with the composition varying based on the contents and nature of tender, shall be involved in approving the award recommendation of tenders. The approval process for tender award shall only be considered complete when approval is obtained from all members of the Tender Board. Depending on the contract sum, the circulation of Tender Report for Tender Board approval shall be initiated with or without off-system meeting. The Platform shall be able to accommodate such situations.
(d) The Platform shall check if special approval from the staff of specific grading is necessary, based on the prescribed conditions and pop up an appropriate reminder message when such conditions are noticed. Tentatively, they are expected to be approximately 3 prescribed conditions for special approval, including but not limited to the following:
(i) When the minimum number of submissions received is not achieved.
(ii) When the total award amount of an RFQ has reached the tender threshold.
(iii) When the lowest priced or highest combined scored offer is not selected for the award.
3.8.3. 	The Contractor shall provide a user-friendly way to let Subject Officers and/or F&A Users to select appropriate senior management staff and/or Tender Board members for the approval of individual award recommendations.
3.8.4. Upon approval of the award recommendation, all necessary information, including but not limited to the following, shall be transferred from the Platform to FMS for PO creation and approval:
(a) Unit rate of selected award item(s) of award supplier(s)
(b) Item remarks inputted by award supplier(s) or Internal Users as recorded on the Platform
(c) Currency
(d) Attachments, including query summary, addendum summary, clarification summary, quotation or Technical and Fee Proposals, price comparison or overall assessment summary. While Subject Officers will have the ability to select the documents to be transferred, certain documents will be defaulted in the Platform as mandatory for transferral.
3.8.5. Before transferring the information from the Platform to FMS for PO creation, the Platform shall allow Subject Officers to distribute the quotation amount of a quotation item into relevant PO lines according to the unit rates and corresponding quantity if the quotation item line refers to a group of PR lines from FMS (see Section 3.4.2. for more details).
3.8.6. With system integration with FMS, Subject Officers shall be able to search for the approved award recommendation on the Platform using a specified reference number for PO creation. If supplier does not exist in FMS, supplier record shall be interfaced to FMS Supplier Platform for creation.
3.8.7. Information and attachments from the Platform shall be mapped and automatically filled into appropriate fields / transferred to appropriate locations in FMS. Subject Officer can make necessary edits before submitting the PO for approval.

Here are the available options and features for the e-Form:
The preparer drafts the e-Form. Drafts can be saved automatically to prevent data loss in case of a session timeout.
The e-Form can be saved in PDF and Excel formats.
PDF version: for attaching to emails for review and audit trails.
Excel version: Helps the preparer in creating documents for off-system / external processes.
The e-Form can be submitted for approval by the requestor designated by the preparer. If the requestor is designated, the e-Form can be reviewed, edited, and submitted for approval. Following submission, email notifications are sent to individuals based on the approval route, considering parallel or sequential modes.
Approvers (Reviewers, endorsers, and final approvers) can take actions such as approve, reject, return with feedback(s) or withdraw, provided that specific conditions are met.
The requestor is allowed to revoke approval at any time, enabling them to cancel the approval request before the final approver grants approval.
In cases where approval requests involve budget virement issues, the requestor can decide whether to inform the controlling officer of the budget division/department after approval or seek their endorsement within the approval route before final approval.
If an approval request requires the Tender Board Chairman's endorsement before issuing Tender Invitations, Addendums, Extending the closing date/time, or terminating the tender, the requestor can decide whether to inform the Tender Board Chairman after approval or seek their endorsement within the approval route before final approval.
Change history: The Platform automatically logs each field, capturing the date and time, the changer's details, the field modified, and old/new values.
Amendment history: The preparer manually fills in the summary of changes with each save.
Action History captures every e-Form status change, specifying the date, time, initiator, and new status.
Approval history tracks overall approval status changes, including the date, time, and last approver. It also logs individual actions with the date, time, person's name, action taken (e.g., approve, reject, withdraw), reasons or remarks, and attachments if necessary.
A weekly reminder email is sent to the requestor and current approvers who are pending approval based on the approval route.
