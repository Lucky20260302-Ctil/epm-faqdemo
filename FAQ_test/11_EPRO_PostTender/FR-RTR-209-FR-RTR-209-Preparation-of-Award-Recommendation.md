# FR-RTR-209: FR-RTR-209: Preparation of Award Recommendation

## 需求描述

Requirement ID:	FR-RTR-209	Requirement Type:	RFQ and Tender	Requirements
Parent Requirement #:
None
Description:
- **3.8** Approval of Award Recommendation
    - **3.8.1** Preparation of Award Recommendation
(a) The Contractor shall design and configure the Platform to allow Subject Officers to prepare award recommendations, including but not limited to the following information:
(i) Selection of supplier(s) to be awarded (partial award, i.e. not awarding all quotation items, and awarding more than one supplier shall be allowed for each RFQ / tender).
(ii) Input of award reasons with prescribed choices and a free text field for other reasons for each supplier to be awarded.
(iii) Selection of award item(s), either mandatory or optional, for each supplier to be awarded.
(iv) The Platform shall calculate the award amount of each individual supplier based on the selection as per Item (iii) above.
(v) If the total contract price exceeds a certain amount as specified in the PPG, a Tender Report shall be attached for Tender Board approval.
(vi) The Platform can automatically fill in the prescribed Tender Report with the information obtained from the Platform and it shall allow the Subject Officers / PRO Users to make edits on certain fields before sending it out for approval.
Rationale:
None
Acceptance / Fit Criteria:
Following the completion of the evaluation of all related RFQ / Tender exercises, Procurement Officers are required to complete the Procurement Strategy e-Form (Part II) to proceed with obtaining Approval of Award Recommendation.
The award recommendation will be based on the approved or estimated budget amount of the selected Purchase Requisition (PR) and selected PR Line Items in the Procurement Strategy e-Form (Part I).
Award item(s) must be selected as either mandatory or optional according to the Requisition Item Groups in the RFQ / Tender exercise for each supplier to be awarded.
Award reasons must be provided, along with a free text field for additional justifications for each supplier to be awarded.
The award recommendation will state the selection of supplier(s) to be awarded, allowing for partial awards (i.e., not awarding all items) and allowing multiple suppliers for each RFQ / Tender.
The Platform shall automatically calculate the award amount for each individual supplier based on the award items.
If the total contract price exceeds a specified amount outlined in the PPG, a Tender Report must be attached for Tender Board approval. The Platform allows for the export of the required Tender Report in Word format and supports mail merge using reserved placeholders, as detailed in FR-RTR-210. Procurement Officers can make edits to the Word document before attaching it for approval.
Upon approval, the Platform will issue a request to create Purchase Orders in accordance with the award recommendation.
The e-Form for drafting the Procurement Strategy e-Form (Part II) related to all RFQ / Tender exercises can be used during RFQ / Tender Negotiation:
Here are the available options and features for the e-Form:
The preparer drafts the e-Form. Drafts can be saved automatically to prevent data loss in case of a session timeout.
The e-Form can be saved in PDF and Excel formats.
PDF version: for attaching to emails for review and audit trails.
Excel version: Helps the preparer in creating documents for off-system / external processes.
Change history: The Platform automatically logs each field, capturing the date and time, the changer's details, the field modified, and old/new values.
Amendment history: The preparer manually fills in the summary of changes with each save.
Action History captures every e-Form status change, specifying the date, time, initiator, and new status.
A weekly reminder email is sent to the preparer who have pending tasks to complete.
