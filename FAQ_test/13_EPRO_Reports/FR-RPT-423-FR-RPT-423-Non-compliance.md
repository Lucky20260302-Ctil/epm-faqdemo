# FR-RPT-423: FR-RPT-423: Non-compliance

## 需求描述

Requirement ID:	FR-RPT-423	Requirement Type:	Report Requirements
Parent Requirement #:
None
Description:
D.	Generation of Reports / Documents
- **3.56** Managerial and statistical reports to be generated by the Platform (with different filtering criteria e.g. period, department and service type, etc.) shall include but not limited to the following: (h) Non-compliance Rationale: None Acceptance / Fit Criteria: The Platform supports the following user roles to export the details in Excel: Finance users The Platform exports the following details in Excel: Summary: This worksheet lists the number of RFQ/Tender exercises that have received exception approval, categorized by exception type, by sourcing category, by user group, and by month over a period of three years Raw Data: This worksheet provides the raw data used to create the Summary worksheet. Note: The detailed design will be provided in a later SA&D stage. The exception types are listed below: Assignment of Technical Assessment Panel Members (Minimum requirements, staff of specific grading is necessary). Assignment of RFQ / Tender Opening Team Members (Minimum requirements, staff of specific grading is necessary). Declaration of Conflict of Interest (Issues required to resolve) Change of Bidder/Tenderer. Excluded Bidder/Tenderer due to NDA/NDU. Excluded Bidder/Tenderer due to attendance at the Briefing / Site visit. Dependencies: Report requirements Tailoring Guidelines: None Change History: None
