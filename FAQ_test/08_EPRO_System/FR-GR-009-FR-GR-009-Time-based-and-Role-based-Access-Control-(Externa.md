# FR-GR-009: FR-GR-009: Time-based and Role-based Access Control (External Suppliers)

## 需求描述

Requirement ID:	FR-GR-009	Requirement Type:	General Requirements
Parent Requirement #:
None
Description:
For External Suppliers, specifically suppliers, should be granted access to designated functions and modules based on their assigned user roles or groups.
Rationale:
None
Acceptance / Fit Criteria:
Unregistered suppliers will not have access to the Platform.
Suppliers will be divided into two categories:
Potential suppliers who have recently completed pre-registration.
Registered suppliers who have completed full registration. In case a supplier has a linked account from a previous pre-registration, the account used during pre-registration will be frozen.
The Platform allows a maximum of six login accounts for suppliers.
In the Platform, the master account holds full access rights and has the ability to manage each sub-account. The master account can assign specific access rights to sub-accounts, including the following:
The master account can also create and manage up to five sub-accounts, allowing for flexibility in user management. It is optional for suppliers to have sub-accounts.
To access the Platform, suppliers must have a username and password, along with 2FA using a one-time password (OTP). The username, a 10-digit number generated by the Platform. The OTP, consisting of 9 digits with the first 3 digits as an identifier, is sent to the supplier's registered email address. The OTP is valid for 30 minutes and cannot be reused. If the OTP is not received, it can be resent every 30 seconds.
As part of the login process, suppliers are required to read and confirm their agreement to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy Statement. The declaration will explicitly inform them about the types of Personal Data and Sensitive Data that will be captured. It will also provide information about the purpose of the Platform, their responsibilities, and the code of conduct to be followed during Supplier Registration, and Tender and RFQ exercises.
The Platform to display user names that include the user's title. For example, the display name for Cynthia Li would be Cynthia Li, Director.
