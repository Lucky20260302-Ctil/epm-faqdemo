Description:
    - **3.1.31** The Contractor shall provide professional advice and be responsible for all aspects in relation to the Platform including but not limited to:
Design and development with configuration and/or customisation of the Platform (including optional items, if engaged) according to the requirements stated in the Tender Documents; and
Integration with all necessary existing platform(s) and/or system(s) to meet HKTDC's requirements on Platform functions.
